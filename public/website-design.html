How remote website design works

  1. Brief: share your business, audience, pages, references, budget range and desired launch date.
  2. Structure and direction: agree the page flow, content responsibilities and visual direction before expanding the design.
  3. Build: develop the approved layouts and scoped interactions, including forms or third-party tools where agreed.
  4. Review and launch: check responsive layouts, contact links, metadata and agreed browsers. Confirm domain, hosting ownership and handover responsibilities.

For international projects, we agree a practical review schedule and communication channel. Your proposal defines the scope, revision stages, currency, timeline and support rather than relying on a generic package description.

What to check before hiring a website designer

Look at relevant live work on your own phone. Ask who writes the content, who owns the domain and code, and whether integrations or 3D assets are included. A useful proposal explains the deliverables, review process and what happens after launch.

Compare the complete scope rather than only the headline price. Page count, custom animation, content preparation, integrations and ongoing support affect the work involved. Send your references and requirements for a scoped Brandier Web proposal.

Questions before starting

Can I hire Brandier Web from outside Pakistan?

Yes. We collaborate remotely with international businesses. Share your location and preferred review times with the project brief so we can agree a workable schedule.

How much does a custom website cost?

Cost depends on page count, content, integrations, 3D assets and support. Email your requirements for a proposal with defined deliverables and payment stages.

Will the website work on mobile?

Responsive design is part of our approach. We plan touch interactions and lighter alternatives for complex motion, and agree the target devices and browsers in the project scope.

What SEO work is included?

We can scope page structure, descriptive titles, crawlable content, canonical URLs, sitemap and relevant structured data. Content strategy and ongoing SEO should be explicitly included in the proposal. Search positions and AI citations cannot be guaranteed.
